Case Study 4 • Python OSINT & Socket Engine

Problem & Vision

Commercial B2B database subscriptions (Apollo, ZoomInfo) cost thousands of dollars annually and carry stale contacts for regional Indian IT corridors. We engineered a proprietary, zero-cost lead pipeline targeting decision-makers (HR Heads, Facility Managers) in Pune IT Parks.

Technical Implementation

  • Anti-Bot OSINT Engine: Extracted company metadata across Hinjewadi Phase 1-3, Magarpatta, and Kharadi EON Free Zone.
  • Live Port 25 SMTP Handshakes: Connects to MX mailboxes executing HELO ➔ MAIL FROM ➔ RCPT TO to verify mailbox existence without sending actual emails.
  • Gemini GenAI: Cleaned contact titles and filtered non-decision-makers.
